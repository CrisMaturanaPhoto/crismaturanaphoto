Hoi An before sunrise has a stillness that is hard to describe but easy to feel. It teaches patience.

I arrive while it is still dark, walking without camera pressure, letting my eyes adjust to quiet transitions in color and contrast.

By the time the first warm light reaches the street walls, every frame is already shaped by observation, not urgency.
